# Pact Azimuth Indicator
A GHPC mod that redesigns the "turret clock" to resemble the typical Soviet azimuth indicator, complete with readout in milliradians  
A full rotation is divided into 6000 mils, written in a XX-XX notation, and oriented such that 00-00 is at 6 o'clock, progressing clockwise.
Just like the real azimuth indicator, the "hours" are in 500 mil intervals, providing 12 positions around the face just like a clock.  

The configuration file allows for displaying only the mil numbers or have the dial converted as well, enlarging the dial for readability, applying the effect to all vehicles or only Warsaw Pact ones, and having commander-designated targets shown on the dial (as an orange 'hand' on the clock) instead of on the compass at the top of the screen.
